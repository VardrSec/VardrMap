# v0.35.0 — 2026-10-07

## Relicensed to AGPL-3.0

VardrMap moves from the MIT license to the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0**.

- **`LICENSE`** replaced with the verbatim AGPL-3.0 text.
- **README** gains a `## License` section: copyright notice, a plain-language summary of the AGPL §13 network-use obligation, and a note that the sole copyright holder may also offer commercial terms.
- **`CONTRIBUTING.md`** added, requiring a **Developer Certificate of Origin** sign-off (`git commit -s`) on every contribution. The `DCO.txt` (Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1) is included. Sign-off keeps dual-licensing possible by confirming each contribution may be redistributed under both the AGPL and any separate commercial license.

### Why

VardrMap is run as a network service, so the AGPL — not plain GPL — is the license that prevents a third party from hosting a modified, closed version: §13 requires anyone who offers a modified VardrMap over a network to publish their corresponding source. The project stays fully open to use, study, and self-hosting. VardrRunner remains MIT so the client stays easy to adopt; VardrGate remains Apache-2.0.

No code, endpoints, models, env vars, or behavior change in this release.
